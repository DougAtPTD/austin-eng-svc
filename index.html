Engineering Services

Automotive — Custom Fabrication

2019–2023 Ford Ranger Brake Controller Mount

Designed and fabricated a custom dashboard-mounted brake controller installation solution for the Ford Ranger platform. The project required creating precision mounting brackets to seamlessly integrate aftermarket braking equipment into the factory dashboard without modification to the vehicle.

CAD Design Bracket Fabrication Dashboard Integration

Kit Car — Electronics Design

Factory Five Type 65 Electronics Mounting

Using 3D scanning technology and professional wiring expertise, designed complete positioning and mounting solutions for the main electronics in a Factory Five Type 65 Kit Car. Included harness design validation and structural analysis of all mounting brackets for reliability under vibration.

3D Scanning Wiring Design FEA Analysis Harness Validation

Motorsport — Chassis Design

2022 West Virginia Formula SAE Chassis

Designed the SAE series' first hybrid steel and titanium chassis to improve serviceability and leverage lighter materials without sacrificing critical characteristics like roll stiffness. Featured a titanium subframe engineered to separate for engine servicing without full removal.

Titanium Hybrid Materials Chassis Design FSAE

About

Austin Smith

I'm a multidisciplinary product development engineer based in the Chicago suburbs, specializing in custom and aftermarket automotive products. My work spans the entire product lifecycle — from reverse engineering existing components through design, fabrication, and final validation.

Every product I develop is built with durability and performance as the primary design criteria. Whether it's a precision mounting bracket, a full wiring harness, or a competition chassis, I bring the same rigorous engineering approach to every project.

With experience across 3D scanning, CAD, FEA, electrical design, and hands-on fabrication, I offer truly end-to-end product development capability under one roof.
